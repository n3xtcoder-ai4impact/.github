## 🧩 Programme Challenges

### 1. Transforming Digital Family Support in Germany

**Partner**: Elternleben.de

**Challenge**: Online counseling is effective but very manual, causing delays in matters that need direct support with children and teenagers.

**Goal**: Develop an AI-powered chatbot that will reduce waiting times, streamline access to information, and free up experts for complex cases.

**Open for**: AI/ML Engineers, Backend & Frontend Developers, UI/UX/Service Designers, Solution Architects, Innovators passionate about family well-being, AI, and ethical technology.

![Family Support Challenge](https://n3xtcoder.org/media/elternleben-banner.jpg)

### 2. Setting the Standard for a Circular Construction Industry in Germany

**Partner**: Federal Institute for Research on Building, Urban Affairs and Spatial Development (BBSR)

**Challenge**: The construction industry drives 40% of global CO₂ emissions and generates over 30% of waste.

**Goal**: Create an AI-enabled MVP that streamlines the assessment of circular materials in building components, promoting sustainable architecture and reducing environmental impact.

**Open for**: Architects, engineers, and sustainability specialists, Data scientists and AI/ML experts, UX designers and product developers, Enthusiasts passionate about climate change and innovation.

![Circular Construction Challenge](https://n3xtcoder.org/media/bbsr-challenge.jpg)
